A CALMER PROMOTION WORKFLOW

From release details
to considered action.

Promotion can feel scattered across links, forms, deadlines, and messages. NOTE is designed to bring the work into one understandable flow.

01

Keep the facts together

Build a reusable Song Passport for the track: release details, contributors, links, and your own rights assertions.

02

Choose with context

Review why an opportunity may fit, where its evidence comes from, what the route requires, and what still needs checking.

03

See what happened

Keep activity, replies, placements, usage, and revenue distinct, with a record that makes the next decision easier.

BUILT AROUND ARTIST CONTROL

Good promotion
starts with clear boundaries.

NOTE is being designed to help independent artists find and manage relevant promotion without turning their music into a numbers game.

Read the product principles ?
01
Legitimate activity

No bots, fake engagement, guaranteed stream schemes, or indiscriminate outreach.

02
Artist-approved boundaries

Spending, paid placements, licences, agreements, and rights decisions need separate approval.

03
Honest outcomes

Activity is not a reply. A reply is not a placement. A placement is not revenue.

04
No artistic gatekeeping

Promotion should not decide whether your music is worthy of being heard.
